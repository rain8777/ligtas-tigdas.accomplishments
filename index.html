    About this dashboard

    LIGTAS TIGDAS is the Philippine Department of Health’s nationwide measles-rubella supplemental immunization activity (MR-SIA) for children 6–59 months. This dashboard tracks daily accomplishment per province and LGU of Region V (Bicol).

    Data: Projected population 6–59 months and daily MR administered figures come from the region’s Ligtas Tigdas 2026 Google Sheet database. The dashboard reads the sheet live when published; otherwise it falls back to the local data/accomplishment.js sample.

    Columns follow the official report layout: 15 campaign days (Aug 10–14, Aug 17–21, Aug 24–28), weekly totals, weekly deferred & refusal. Only data up to the current date is shown; future campaign days are excluded. Daily & weekly percentages are computed against the projected population.

    Boundaries: PSA PSGC via the open-source philippines-json-maps project. Boundary lines are approximations and not legal or survey-grade.
